Question étudiée Résumé PubMed (texte source)

OBJECTIVE To describe the ophthalmic examination protocol within the German National Cohort (NAKO)/NAKO Gesundheitsstudie, to report the baseline profile of participants undergoing ophthalmic assessment, and to illustrate the potential as a population-based resource for artificial intelligence (AI) research in eye health.

Type d'étude et méthode Résumé PubMed (texte source)PubMed (types de publication)

Type(s) de publication déclaré(s) par PubMed : Journal Article

DESIGN Baseline analysis of ophthalmic data within the nationwide, population-based multicenter prospective NAKO study.
PARTICIPANTS Forty-eight thousand four hundred and sixty adults in the ophthalmic level 2 module of 205 053 adults enrolled in NAKO, aged 19-74 years, with a mean age 48.9 ± 12.5 years, and 52.7% were male.
METHODS All participants underwent standardized biomedical examinations and detailed questionnaire-based data collection, including nondilated color fundus imaging, visual acuity testing, and a brief ocular history. Ocular health was summarized using descriptive statistics. Fundus image quality and morphological features (e.g., cup-to-disc ratio and arteriolar-to-venular ratio) were assessed using open-source deep learning models. Standard deep learning architectures were trained on the fundus images to predict age, sex, and blood pressure.
MAIN OUTCOME MEASURES Percentage of fundus images graded as good quality; mean absolute error (MAE) for age and blood pressure prediction; accuracy for sex prediction.

RESULTS The analysis includes 48 460 participants who completed the ophthalmic baseline examination of NAKO. Mean visual acuity (logarithm of the minimum angle of resolution) was 0.01 ± 0.20 (left eye, mean ± standard deviation) and 0.03 ± 0.21 (right eye). Self-reported ocular disease prevalence was 4.2% for cataract, 2.0% for glaucoma, and 0.9% for macular degeneration; 68.2% of fundus images were classified as gradable using 4 deep learning-based quality grading models. Morphological features showed systematic differences across age groups. Standard deep learning architectures showed comparative performance to the state-of-the-art for age, sex, and blood pressure prediction (2.96 MAE for age prediction, 0.84 accuracy for sex prediction, and 10.78 and 6.97 MAE for systolic and diastolic blood pressure prediction).

Conclusion des auteurs (citée telle quelle, non évaluée par l'outil) Résumé PubMed (texte source)

CONCLUSIONS The German National Cohort provides a large-scale, nationwide population-based resource with visual acuity measurements and systemic health indicators, as well as color fundus images in about 50 000 NAKO participants. These data lay the groundwork for studying eye health in the general adult population in Germany and can serve as a strong foundation for developing and validating AI tools in eye health research.
