Résultats disponibles Résumé PubMed (texte source)

Valeurs chiffrées surlignées telles qu'elles figurent dans le résumé. Elles n'ont pas été vérifiées dans le texte intégral.

RESULTS POH occurred in 22/63 (35%) eyes (46% of pseudophakic and 26% of aphakic eyes), glaucoma in 21/62 (34%) eyes (52% of pseudophakic and 20% of aphakic eyes), and vision loss in 16/61 (26%) eyes (36% of pseudophakic and 18% of aphakic eyes). Only 3 cases of retinal detachment precluded further analysis. Estimated hazard ratios (pseudophakia compared to aphakia, unadjusted and adjusted for year of surgery) were, respectively, 2.0 (95% CI = 0.8 to 5.5; p = 0.157) to 2.8 (95% CI = 1.0 to 7.9; p = 0.052) for POH and 2.9 (95% CI = 0.9 to 9.2; p = 0.075) to 2.7 (95% CI = 1.0 to 7.3; p = 0.050) for glaucoma. Estimated effects of lens status on vision loss and effects of PTR on all three outcomes were imprecise and inconclusive.
