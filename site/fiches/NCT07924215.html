Question étudiée Registre ClinicalTrials.gov

Titre officiel : Clinical Outcomes, Contrast Sensitivity, and Patient Satisfaction Following Bilateral PureSee EDOF Versus ZCB00 Monofocal IOL Implantation in Patients With Mild to Moderate Primary Open-Angle Glaucoma: An Ambispective Investigator-Initiated Trial

This study is a single center, ambispective study of visual outcomes after successful bilateral cataract surgery in patients with mild to moderate primary open-angle glaucoma (POAG). Two cohorts are compared: patients with bilateral implantation of the TECNIS PureSee non-diffractive extended depth of focus intraocular lens (EDOF IOL) and patients with bilateral implantation of the TECNIS ZCB00 monofocal IOL.

Critères d'inclusion et d'exclusion (texte du registre)
Inclusion Criteria:

Age ≥45 years. Prior bilateral cataract surgery with bilateral implantation of either PureSee EDOF or TECNIS ZCB00 on or after March 12, 2026.

Intended postoperative refractive target between plano and -0.50 D in each eye. No intentional monovision. Bilateral mild or moderate POAG according to AAO ICD-10 staging criteria. Reliable glaucoma visual field available within approximately 18 months before cataract surgery or study enrollment, when available.

Reliable optical coherence tomography examination within 6 months. Adequately controlled IOP and clinically stable glaucoma in the investigator's opinion.

Prior SLT is permitted. Concurrent MIGS at the time of cataract surgery is permitted when clinically indicated at the discretion of the treating surgeon.

Ability to complete the study-specific examination and questionnaires. Study visit occurring 3-18 months following second-eye cataract surgery.

Exclusion Criteria:

Severe POAG or glaucomatous visual field loss involving fixation. Secondary glaucoma, including pseudoexfoliative, pigmentary, angle-closure, neovascular, or uveitic glaucoma.

Glaucoma surgery other than eligible concurrent MIGS that may materially influence study outcomes.

Corneal pathology or irregular astigmatism affecting visual outcomes. Prior corneal refractive surgery. Macular or neuro-ophthalmic disease affecting visual function. Other ocular pathology expected to limit postoperative CDVA to worse than 20/30.

Inability to obtain reliable visual field testing when required for glaucoma characterization.

Intentional monovision. The principal investigator reserves the right to declare a patient ineligible or non-evaluable based on medical evidence that indicates they are unsuitable for the trial.

Critères évalués (tels que déclarés) Registre ClinicalTrials.gov

  • Principal : Distance visual acuity - binocular distance-corrected visual acuity (DCVA) [3 to 18 months following second-eye cataract surgery]
  • Secondaire : Intermediate visual acuity - binocular distance-corrected intermediate visual acuity (DCIVA) [3 to 18 months following second-eye cataract surgery]
