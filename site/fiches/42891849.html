Question étudiée Résumé PubMed (texte source)

PURPOSE To evaluate stage-dependent differences in mean deviation (MD) recalculated from the 24plus(1-2) and 24plus(1) test locations and to identify the MD range at which divergence between the reduced and full location sets exceeds a prespecified operational threshold in glaucoma.

Type d'étude et méthode Résumé PubMed (texte source)PubMed (types de publication)

Type(s) de publication déclaré(s) par PubMed : Journal Article

METHODS This retrospective, cross-sectional study included 394 eyes from 197 patients with glaucoma including preperimetric glaucoma (PPG). The 24plus(1) MD was recalculated from a subset of 36 test locations derived from the 24plus(1-2) grid. Glaucoma severity was classified as PPG, early, moderate, or advanced based on MD. Differences between the full and reduced location sets were assessed using cluster-adjusted Bland-Altman analysis and linear mixed-effects models. Mixed-effects logistic regression was applied to estimate the probability of exceeding a prespecified operational MD-difference threshold (|ΔMD|> 1 dB), with patient-level bootstrap confidence intervals.

RESULTS The overall mean difference in recalculated MD was - 0.52 dB (95% limits of agreement: - 2.82 to 1.78 dB). The proportion of eyes with |ΔMD|> 1 dB was 1.9% in PPG, 17.8% in early glaucoma, 50.0% in moderate glaucoma, and 67.5% in advanced glaucoma. Worse MD was significantly associated with a higher probability of exceeding the prespecified operational MD-difference threshold (β = - 0.169; P < 0.001). MD values of - 5.4 dB (95% CI: - 7.0 to - 4.0) and - 11.9 dB (95% CI: - 14.1 to - 9.9) corresponded to 25% and 50% predicted probabilities of deviation, respectively.
