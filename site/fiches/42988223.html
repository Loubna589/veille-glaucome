Question étudiée Résumé PubMed (texte source)

PURPOSE To determine whether trabeculotomy is noninferior to combined trabeculotomy-trabeculectomy (CTT) for primary congenital glaucoma (PCG) with a horizontal corneal diameter (HCD) of 12-14 mm.

Type d'étude et méthode Résumé PubMed (texte source)PubMed (types de publication)

DESIGN Prospective, randomized, controlled noninferiority trial.
PARTICIPANTS Children from birth through 3 years of age with PCG, a HCD of 12 to 14 mm, and no previous intraocular or laser surgery were eligible. Two hundred and forty-eight children were enrolled from January 2015 through April 2021, and the final follow-up was completed in April 2024.
METHODS One eligible eye per child was randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio to trabeculotomy or CTT.
MAIN OUTCOME MEASURES The primary outcome was qualified surgical success at 36 months, defined as an intraocular pressure (IOP) of 5 to 21 mmHg with or without glaucoma medications, without vision-threatening complications or additional glaucoma surgery. The noninferiority margin was -15 percentage points. Exploratory secondary outcomes included changes in IOP, ocular morphometric and functional outcomes, and adverse events.

RESULTS Overall, 124 children were assigned to trabeculotomy (mean age, 9.8 months; 76.6% male) and 124 to CTT (mean age, 9.9 months; 68.5% male). In the intention-to-treat population, qualified success at 36 months was achieved in 102 of 124 eyes (82.3%) in the trabeculotomy group and 105 of 124 eyes (84.7%) in the CTT group (risk difference: -2.4%; 95%CI, -11.7 to 6.9; Pnoninferiority= 0.004). Corresponding rates in the per-protocol population were 90.3% and 92.9%, respectively (risk difference -2.6%; 95%CI -10.3 to 4.9; Pnoninferiority< 0.001). Mean IOP reduction was -12.1 mm Hg (95%CI, -12.9 to -11.4) and -13.2 mm Hg (95%CI, -14.0 to -12.4) in the trabeculotomy and CTT groups, respectively (P=0.067). No statistically significant between-group differences were observed in other secondary outcomes. Adverse events occurred in 56 children (45.2%) in the trabeculotomy group and 67 (54.0%) in the CTT group (P = 0.162). Hyphema was the most common adverse event in both groups, and most cases resolved spontaneously.

Conclusion des auteurs (citée telle quelle, non évaluée par l'outil) Résumé PubMed (texte source)

CONCLUSIONS Trabeculotomy was noninferior to CTT for qualified surgical success at 36 months in PCG patients with a HCD of 12-14 mm. These findings suggest that trabeculotomy may be an alternative to CTT in this population.
