Résultats disponibles Résumé PubMed (texte source)

Valeurs chiffrées surlignées telles qu'elles figurent dans le résumé. Elles n'ont pas été vérifiées dans le texte intégral.

RESULTS Severe vision loss was recorded in 3723 (0.21%) eyes, including 3 eyes (2 patients) from 2 718 immediate sequential bilateral cataract surgery (ISBCS) eyes. The highest observed SVL rate was 10.68% for eyes recorded as left aphakic after surgery and the second highest observed SVL rate (3.32%) was when posterior capsular rupture occurred. The SVL rate was higher than the overall rate for eyes with previous intravitreal injections (0.76%), eyes with optic nerve or central nervous system problems (0.74%), age-related macular degeneration (0.52%), previous vitrectomy surgery (0.49%), diabetic retinopathy (0.45%), glaucoma (0.42%) or corneal pathology (0.33%). Of 1,642,386 (93.8%) eyes that were not preoperatively legally blind, 2731 (0.2%) were postoperatively legally blind. Of these, 2294 (84.0%) eyes experienced severe vision loss.
