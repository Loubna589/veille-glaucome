Question étudiée Résumé PubMed (texte source)

BACKGROUND The objective of this study was to investigate the correlation between papillomacular bundle (PMB) thickness, measured using a single optical coherence tomography (OCT) vertical B-scan one papillary diameter temporal from the optic disc, and the corresponding visual field (VF) sector composed of 10 - 2 examination points of static automated perimetry (SAP). The goal was to visualize this relationship as a structure-function map.

Type d'étude et méthode Résumé PubMed (texte source)PubMed (types de publication)

Type(s) de publication déclaré(s) par PubMed : Journal Article

METHODS A total of 205 eyes from 125 patients who visited the glaucoma clinic of Kyoto University Hospital between 2015 and 2017 were included. The SAP 10 - 2 examination points were clustered into 3-5 VF sectors using explanatory factor analysis. Retinal nerve fiber layers (RNFL) in the OCT B-scan images were divided into peripapillary vertical RNFL segments (PVRSs) at 5° intervals. The correlation between PVRS thickness and mean sensitivity in each VF sector was examined using linear mixed models. RNFL-VF maps were then constructed, and the PMB range was identified.
