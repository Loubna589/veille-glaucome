RESULTS The incidences of cataracts, glaucoma, uveitis, and corneal ulcers were relatively high. Multiple diseases occurred in one eye in 65 rabbits (31.7%) and 80 eyes (26.9%), overall; the most common combination was cataracts and uveitis. Glaucoma incidence increased with age (odds ratio (OR): 1.010, 95% confidence interval (CI): 1.000-1.020; p = 0.007) and was less frequent in intact males (OR: 0.295, 95% CI: 0.094-0.927; p = 0.037) and spayed females (OR: 0.163, 95% CI: 0.042-0.638; p = 0.009) compared with neutered males. Uveitis incidence decreased with age (OR: 0.970, 95% CI: 0.958-0.982; p < 0.001; median: 1 year), and corneal ulcer incidence increased with age (OR: 1.010, 95% CI: 1.000-1.020; p = 0.007; median: 4 years). Holland Lops (73 rabbits, 116 eyes) and Netherland Dwarfs (63 rabbits, 82 eyes) were the two most frequently represented breeds. In Holland Lops, glaucoma and cataracts accounted for more than half of the ophthalmic diseases. In Netherland Dwarfs, the most common diseases were uveitis, corneal ulcers, tarsal plate abnormality, and glaucoma.
