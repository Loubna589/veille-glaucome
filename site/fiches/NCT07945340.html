Question étudiée Registre ClinicalTrials.gov

Titre officiel : Boosting Retinal Impulses in Glaucoma With a Holistic Therapeutic Approach: a Randomized, Placebo-controlled, Electrophysiology-based Clinical Trial. The BRIGHT Study

The goal of this clinical trial is to learn whether a daily dietary supplement called iNAD CONTROL can improve the function of the retinal ganglion cells (the nerve cells of the retina that carry visual information to the brain) in adults with open-angle glaucoma who are already being treated with eye-pressure-lowering therapy. iNAD CONTROL contains nicotinamide riboside (a form of vitamin B3), acetyl-L-carnitine, forskolin and hesperidin, which may support the energy metabolism of these nerve cells and protect them from damage. The main question it aims to answer is: \- Does taking iNAD CONTROL for 12 weeks improve retinal ganglion cell function, measured with an electroretinogram (ERG, a test that records the electrical response of the retina to light), compared with a placebo (a look-alike tablet with no active ingredients)? Researchers will also look at whether iNAD CONTROL changes the visual field, the thickness of the retinal nerve fiber layers measured with optical coherence tomography (OCT), visual acuity and eye pressure. Researchers will compare iNAD CONTROL with placebo. Each participant will receive both: iNAD CONTROL for 12 weeks and placebo for 12 weeks, in a random order. Neither the participants nor the study doctors will know which product is being taken in each period. Participants will: * Take 1 tablet a day for 24 weeks in total (12 weeks of iNAD CONTROL and 12 weeks of placebo) * Continue their usual glaucoma eye drops without changes * Attend 5 clinic visits over 6 months (at the start and at weeks 6, 12, 18 and 24) * At each visit, undergo non-invasive eye tests: visual acuity, eye pressure, visual field tests, ERG and OCT, plus blood pressure and body weight measurement

Description de l'insu (texte du registre)

iNAD CONTROL and placebo tablets are matched in appearance and organoleptic characteristics and are packaged and labelled identically. Participants, investigators, outcome assessors, study coordinators, data managers and statisticians involved in the primary analysis remain masked to treatment allocation throughout the study. ERG waveforms are processed centrally by masked assessors. Emergency unblinding is permitted only when knowledge of the assigned treatment is essential for participant safety.

Critères d'inclusion et d'exclusion (texte du registre)
Inclusion Criteria:

* Age 18 years or older
* Freely given written informed consent obtained prior to any study procedure
* Capacity to understand and willingness to follow study instructions, and likely to complete all required visits and procedures
* Diagnosis of open-angle glaucoma under IOP-lowering treatment
* Visual field mean deviation (MD) between -3.0 and -12.0 dB
* Best-corrected visual acuity of 8/10 (0.1 logMAR) or better
* Spherical refraction between +3 and -4 diopters and astigmatism within ±3 diopters
* IOP below 18 mmHg at the 2 previous visits and at the screening visit
* Stable IOP-lowering regimen planned for the next 6 months, as judged by the investigator
* Reliable Humphrey 24-2 visual field at screening/baseline (fixation losses and false negatives below 15%, false positives below 10%)
* Reproducible glaucomatous visual field defect, defined as Glaucoma Hemifield Test outside normal limits and a cluster of 3 or more locations on the pattern deviation plot, 2 of which are depressed at P\<5% and 1 at P\<1%, on at least 2 consecutive reliable fields, with at least 1 common location in the cluster depressed at P\<5% in both fields (30-2 or 24-2 tests may be used, considering only the 24-2 locations)
* At least 3 visual field examinations performed in the 3 years prior to enrollment, to minimize learning effects
* Women of childbearing potential must agree to use an appropriate contraceptive method and have a negative urine pregnancy test on the first day of dosing

Only one eye per participant is designated as the study eye. If both eyes are eligible, the eye with the better quality visual field at screening (lower fixation losses and false-positive rates) is selected. Ocular inclusion criteria (MD, IOP, visual acuity, refraction, astigmatism) apply to the study eye only; the fellow eye must not meet any exclusion criterion that could confound interpretation of results.

Exclusion Criteria:

* Intraocular surgery within the previous 6 months, or surgery planned during the study
* Coexisting retinal or optic nerve disease (e.g., non-glaucomatous optic neuropathies of ischemic, inflammatory or compressive origin), moderate-to-severe diabetic retinopathy, advanced macular degeneration, or advanced cataract likely to confound ERG, visual field or OCT interpretation
* Use of any other systemic or topical agent with potential neuroprotective effects (e.g., citicoline) within the previous month or planned during the study
* Changes in IOP-lowering therapy within the previous 3 months
* Pregnancy or breastfeeding
* Known allergy to nicotinamide riboside, acetyl-L-carnitine, forskolin, hesperidin or any excipient
* Clinically significant hepatic disease or active gastrointestinal ulcer disease
* Severe renal impairment (e.g., eGFR below 30 mL/min/1.73 m²) or other condition judged by the investigator to increase risk
* History of cancer within the past 5 years
