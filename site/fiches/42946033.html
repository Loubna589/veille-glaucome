Type d'étude et méthode Résumé PubMed (texte source)PubMed (types de publication)

Type(s) de publication déclaré(s) par PubMed : Journal Article

DESIGN Prospective, randomized, parallel-group clinical trial.
METHODS Sixty eyes of 60 adults with medically uncontrolled glaucoma and visually significant cataracts underwent PE-DS with an autologous anterior capsule spacer (Spacer group) or PE-DS alone (Control group). Follow-up was at least 12 months. The primary outcome was change in intraocular pressure (IOP) from baseline through 12 months. Secondary outcomes included glaucoma medication use, surgical success according to prespecified IOP-based criteria, and adverse events.

RESULTS Baseline IOP was similar (25.14 ± 9.62 vs 23.77 ± 5.18 mmHg) and both study groups had significant IOP reduction at all visits. IOP was significantly lower in the spacer group at month 6 (11.90 ± 1.82 vs 13.13 ± 2.20 mmHg; P = 0.022) and month 12 (12 ± 2.20 vs 13.29 ± 2.68 mmHg; P = 0.042). Medication use was lower in eyes with an autologous spacer at months 6 (0.14 ± 0.45 vs 0.48 ± 0.85; P= 0.063) and 12 (0.29 ± 0.66 vs 0.68 ± 1.04; P =0.094). Under stringent success definitions, survival favored the spacer (e.g., Definition C: IOP≤12 mmHg: 47.5% vs 15.5%; log-rank test P = 0.025).

Conclusion des auteurs (citée telle quelle, non évaluée par l'outil) Résumé PubMed (texte source)

CONCLUSIONS AND RELEVANCE Adding an autologous anterior capsule spacer during PE-DS was associated with lower IOP at 6 and 12 months and higher cumulative success under stringent IOP thresholds. Medication use was lower in the spacer group, although the between-group differences showed a trend toward statistical significance.
