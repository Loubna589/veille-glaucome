Question étudiée Registre ClinicalTrials.gov

Titre officiel : Efficacy and Safety of Minimally Invasive Suprachoroidal Drainage for the Treatment of Advanced Neovascular Glaucoma

This study primarily involved a retrospective analysis of clinical and follow-up data from patients with advanced neovascular glaucoma who underwent minimally invasive suprachoroidal drainage surgery at the Department of Ophthalmology, Zhongnan Hospital of Wuhan University, to evaluate the safety and efficacy of this procedure in patients with advanced neovascular glaucoma.

Critères d'inclusion et d'exclusion (texte du registre)
Inclusion Criteria:

* Patients who met the diagnostic criteria for advanced NVG.

Exclusion Criteria:

* Patients with incomplete clinical data and information.
* Severe systemic diseases (such as chronic renal failure requiring dialysis, severe neurological diseases, immune diseases), pregnancy or lactation.
