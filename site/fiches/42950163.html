Résultats disponibles Résumé PubMed (texte source)

RESULTS Between 2015 and 2024, 344,370 SLT procedures were recorded. Rates rose from 127 to 150 per 100,000 (β = 1,335 procedures/year; 95% CI 969-1,701; p < 0.001). Annual glaucoma medication prescriptions decreased from 4,167,417 to 3,390,394 (17,498 to 12,466 per 100,000), without a statistically significant linear trend (β = -52,005/year; 95% CI - 104,373-363; p = 0.051). Overall, 113,466 glaucoma surgeries were recorded: annual procedures increased from 4,953 (20.8 per 100,000) to 15,334 (56.4 per 100,000). Minimally invasive glaucoma surgeries (MIGS) increased from 2,177 in 2017 to 10,501 in 2024, compared with 4,080 to 4,386 filtration procedures and 292 to 409 drainage device procedures. Negative binomial regression found no significant annual rate trends for MIGS (IRR 1.16; 95% CI 0.86-1.57; p = 0.34), filtration procedures (IRR 1.00; 95% CI 0.81-1.24; p = 0.97), or drainage devices (IRR 1.03; 95% CI 0.83-1.28; p = 0.79). Optometrists' share of glaucoma prescriptions increased from 1.25% to 5.63%.

CONCLUSIONS Our findings demonstrate changing glaucoma treatment trends in Australia. SLT and MIGS procedure volumes increased as medication prescriptions declined, while optometrists accounted for an increasing proportion of medication prescribing.
