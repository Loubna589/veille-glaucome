Type d'étude et méthode Résumé PubMed (texte source)PubMed (types de publication)

Type(s) de publication déclaré(s) par PubMed : Journal Article

METHODS Anterior chamber angles (ACAs) were classified as PAS or non-PAS (NPAS) groups by indentation gonioscopy. A custom-built air-puff dynamic anterior segment optical coherence tomography (DAS-OCT) system with an innovative 4-leaf clover-scan pattern acquired 10 frames per ACA along 8 radial directions at 45-degree intervals within 65 ms. Dynamic parameters including the differences in angle opening distance at 750 µm from the scleral spur (AOD750) and trabecular-iris space area at 750 µm anterior to the scleral spur (TISA750) before and after air-puff stimulation, were extracted to establish a binary logistic regression model. Model performance was evaluated in the testing set.

RESULTS Analysis of 68 images from 9 healthy eyes confirmed high measurement repeatability (intraclass correlation coefficient [ICC] = 0.821 for AOD750dif; 0.775 for TISA750dif). A total of 143 anterior segment OCT (AS-OCT) images from 20 ACG eyes were analyzed, with 100 assigned to the training set and 43 to the testing set. Significant differences in AOD750dif and TISA750dif were identified between the NPAS and PAS groups (P < 0.001). The dual-parameter model achieved an area under the curve (AUC) of 0.924 in the training set and 0.913 in the testing set, with an overall accuracy of 83.7%.
